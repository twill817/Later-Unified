# Typography

One family, Inter, for everything. Hierarchy comes from size and weight, not from mixing faces. Numbers use tabular figures so columns line up.

| `--lo-font-size-xs` | 11.5px | 400 | Captions and chart axes |

## Writing

- Sentence case everywhere, including buttons and headings.
- Buttons say what happens: "Approve offers," and the confirmation says "Offers approved."
- Name things the way people do: "Inbox," not "conversation queue."
- Errors say what went wrong and how to fix it, without apologizing.
